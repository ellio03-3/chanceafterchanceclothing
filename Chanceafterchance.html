Built on God’s grace and mercy.

Chanceafterchance started from a lyric in “God I’m Just Grateful for You.” That lyric inspired a faith-based clothing brand, made as a reminder of Jesus’ sacrifice, His mercy and grace. Not to be abused or taken advantage of, but to lead us on to the right path.

Each design is a reminder for a hard morning: you are forgiven, you are loved, and you get to begin again. Wear it on the days you feel strong, and on the days you do not.

Made to be worn and to start conversations.
